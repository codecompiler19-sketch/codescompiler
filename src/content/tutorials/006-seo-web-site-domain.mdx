# Website Domains and SEO

A domain name is the human-readable address people use to reach a website, such as `example.com`. It is part of your brand and helps visitors recognize and return to your site. A domain choice can affect trust and usability, but adding a keyword to a domain does not guarantee higher search rankings.

## Choose a domain people can remember

A practical domain is usually:

- easy to spell and say aloud;
- distinctive enough to associate with your brand;
- suitable for your audience and long-term plans;
- free from confusing hyphens or unnecessary wording where possible;
- available for registration and appropriate for your intended use.

For example, a business called “Riverbend Baking” might prefer a clear brand domain over a long address such as `best-cheap-birthday-cakes-in-delhi.example`. The shorter option is easier to mention and can support future products beyond one keyword.

## What about domain extensions?

Extensions such as `.com`, `.org`, or a country-code extension serve different naming and organizational purposes. Choose one that fits your audience, identity, and availability. Do not select an extension solely because you believe it guarantees a ranking advantage.

## Domain names and keywords

A relevant word in a domain may help people understand what a business does, but it is not a substitute for helpful pages, clear navigation, and a reliable service. Avoid choosing an awkward or misleading domain simply to include several search phrases.

## Keep URLs stable

The domain is only one part of a page URL. For example:

```text
https://example.com/guides/image-compression/
```

Here, `https://` is the scheme, `example.com` is the domain, and `/guides/image-compression/` is the path.

If you change a domain or move pages, plan the migration carefully. Map old URLs to the most relevant new destinations, use permanent redirects where appropriate, update internal links and canonical references, and verify the new site in search tools. Avoid redirecting every old URL to the homepage when a closer replacement exists.

## Subdomains and subdirectories

A subdomain such as `docs.example.com` and a subdirectory such as `example.com/docs/` are both valid ways to organize content. Choose based on architecture, ownership, and maintenance needs rather than assuming one structure always ranks better.

## Quick checklist

Before registering or changing a domain, confirm spelling, ownership, renewal access, HTTPS setup, redirects if migrating, and consistency across important references. Keep the brand recognizable and make the site useful after visitors arrive.
